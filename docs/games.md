# Games and lessons

## Neon Dungeon: Rogue Crawler

Build a top-down dungeon game through canvas drawing, movement, wall collisions, and scoring.

1. [Canvas and player sprite](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-1?game=neon-dungeon)
2. [Velocity and movement](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-2?game=neon-dungeon)
3. [Wall collision math](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-3?game=neon-dungeon)
4. [Game loop, enemies, and score](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-4?game=neon-dungeon)

## Cyber Invaders: Space Grid

Build an arcade space defender through ship drawing, movement and lasers, alien grids, collisions, bunkers, and scoring.

1. [Space canvas and defense ship](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-1?game=cyber-invaders)
2. [Strafing and plasma lasers](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-2?game=cyber-invaders)
3. [Alien grid and collision math](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-3?game=cyber-invaders)
4. [Game loop, bunkers, and score](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/tutor/step-4?game=cyber-invaders)

Every lesson has `TODO` tasks, progressive hints, a worked example, and checks. [Explore the crawlable lesson guide](https://pat.pcssiirobotics.org/discover/) for descriptions of each step.
